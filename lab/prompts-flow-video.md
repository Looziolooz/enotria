# Œnotria — 23 clip per Google Flow (Veo) · catena completa

Da usare DOPO l'approvazione dei 24 keyframe (`prompts-gemini-keyframes.md`).

**Regola della catena (v4):** ogni coppia di keyframe consecutivi è una clip —
prima→seconda, seconda→terza, terza→quarta… **Nessuno stacco**: anche i cambi di
scena sono clip-ponte in cui Veo genera il viaggio da un quadro all'altro. Così ogni
fotogramma del film appartiene a due clip (ultimo dell'una, primo dell'altra) e il
contesto non si perde mai.

Per ogni clip, in Flow:
1. Modalità **Frames to Video**: carica il **primo** e l'**ultimo** fotogramma indicati.
2. Incolla il prompt di movimento.
3. Formato 16:9. Veo genera ~8s: le clip di scena si tengono lunghe, le clip-ponte
   (marcate **PONTE**) si trimmano corte (2–3s) al montaggio.
4. **Ignorare l'audio generato**: il film è muto.
5. Stessa sessione/progetto Flow per tutte e 23: meno deriva di resa.

Regole in ogni prompt: un solo gesto, passo cerimoniale lento, camera ferma o una
sola mossa, texture pittorica stabile («painterly texture stable, no flicker»).

## Clip 1 — La nave si avvicina
**Primo:** KF-1.A · **Ultimo:** KF-1.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The Greek ship glides slowly from the far
horizon toward the middle of the canvas across flat teal water bands, its off-white
square sail swelling gently. The pale gold meander rings over the sky drift outward
very slowly. Slow ceremonial pace, locked camera, painterly texture stable, no
flicker, no new elements appear.
```

## Clip 2 — L'approdo · PONTE
**Primo:** KF-1.B · **Ultimo:** KF-2.A

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The Greek ship sails on toward the right and
gently beaches on a pale shore that slides into frame at the right edge; the sail
lowers, and tiny robed figures step out and begin unloading. At the far left, the
first slopes of a terraced vineyard hill appear. The figures are tiny, one sixth of
the frame height. Slow continuous motion, locked camera, painterly texture stable,
no flicker.
```

## Clip 4 — Dentro la vigna · PONTE (dal lontano al vicino)
**Primo:** KF-2.B · **Ultimo:** KF-3.A

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. One slow continuous camera glide: from the
wide view of the shore the camera travels left and upward over the low dry-stone

```

## Clip 5 — Davanti al grappolo

## Clip 6 — La raccolta

## Clip 8 — Lo scambio del grappolo
**Primo:** KF-5.A · **Ultimo:** KF-5.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. All movement in this shot is DOWNWARD: the
```

## Clip 11 — La pigiatura
**Primo:** KF-7.A · **Ultimo:** KF-7.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. All movement in this shot is DOWNWARD: the
```

## Clip 12 — L'ingresso del cantiniere · PONTE

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The scene holds almost still — the woman
```

## Clip 13 — L'anfora spalleggiata
**Primo:** KF-8.A · **Ultimo:** KF-8.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. Two movements only: first UPWARD — the older

```

## Clip 14 — Attraverso la porta · PONTE
**Primo:** KF-8.B · **Ultimo:** KF-9.A

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The camera follows the older man as he walks
into the dark doorway: the deep teal shadow of the threshold fills the frame for a

fully upright inside a single shaft of warm golden light, the amphora on his
shoulder, buried amphorae emerging from the earth around him. One continuous

```

## Clip 15 — L'anfora offerta
**Primo:** KF-9.A · **Ultimo:** KF-9.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The old man stands fully upright in the shaft
of warm golden light, and in one slow ceremonial movement lowers the amphora from

slightly forward, like an offering. He remains standing straight the whole time,
never kneeling. Dust motes drift in the light shaft. Very slow, locked camera,

```

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The old man holds the amphora before his
```

## Clip 17 — Lo scambio fra i mondi
**Primo:** KF-10.A · **Ultimo:** KF-10.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The final exchange: the modern winemaker
steps into frame from the right, and the old man, standing upright, passes the
amphora from his hands into the winemaker's open hands; all four hands hold it
together in the shaft of light. As they do, rows of modern oak barrels emerge gently
from the teal shadow behind the winemaker at the right, while the buried amphorae

standing. Locked camera, painterly texture stable, no flicker.
```

## Clip 18 — Verso le botti · PONTE
**Primo:** KF-10.B · **Ultimo:** KF-11.A

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. One slow continuous move: the modern
```

## Clip 19 — Il travaso
**Primo:** KF-11.A · **Ultimo:** KF-11.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. All movement in this shot is DOWNWARD: the
winemaker tilts the ancient amphora over the open barrel and the ruby-red wine flows

```

## Clip 20 — Al rubinetto · PONTE
**Primo:** KF-11.B · **Ultimo:** KF-12.A

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. One slow continuous move: the camera glides
down and to the left, closer to the head of the barrel, until the small brass

```

## Clip 21 — Il calice alzato
**Primo:** KF-12.A · **Ultimo:** KF-12.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. First DOWNWARD, then UPWARD: the thin stream
of ruby-red wine runs from the barrel's brass tap down into the stemmed glass; the
stream stops, and he raises the full glass up into the warm golden light, where thin
pale-gold meander rings ripple outward from the glass through the teal shadow, like
a slow breath — the same rings that opened the film. Locked camera, one single
rising gesture at the end, painterly texture stable, no flicker.
```

## Clip 22 — Il sorso · PONTE
**Primo:** KF-12.B · **Ultimo:** KF-13.A

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. One slow continuous move: the camera pulls
back and rises slightly, widening from the raised glass to include the winemaker's
head and shoulders in profile facing left, as he brings the glass to his lips and
begins to drink calmly, eyes closing, the warm golden light on his face, the oak
barrels in the teal shadow behind. One smooth reframe, no cuts, painterly texture
stable, no flicker.
```

## Clip 23 — Dentro il vino
**Primo:** KF-13.A · **Ultimo:** KF-13.B

```
A neoclassical oil painting in motion. The winemaker lowers the glass a little from
his lips; then the camera performs the ONLY zoom of the whole film: it pushes slowly
and steadily INTO the glass of ruby-red wine, closer and closer, until the wine
fills the entire frame and everything dissolves into an almost flat saturated ruby
color field with the faintest slow swirl, one faint tone-on-tone meander ring
settling in the lower right. Very slow, one single continuous push-in, painterly
texture stable, no flicker.
```

## Dopo le 23 clip

1. Scaricare i 23 mp4 in `_sorgenti/video-v2/` con nomi `01-nave.mp4` …
   `23-dentro-il-vino.mp4` (numerazione = ordine di questa lista).
2. Trim: clip di scena lunghe, clip-ponte corte (2–3s) — il ritmo si decide al
   montaggio, non in generazione.
3. Estrazione a 24 fps (`extract-frames.sh` adattato) → film flat in
   `public/frames/film/` + tier mobile.
4. `node lab/genera-inventario.mjs` per rifare `segmenti.json` e ASSETS.md.
5. `tagli.json`: con la catena completa non servono transizioni shader — il file si
   svuota o resta solo per eventuali correzioni.
6. Registrare tutto in `ASSETS.md`: prompt, data, strumento (nano banana + Flow/Veo).
